SKU Shot

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

SKU Shot is an iOS app for scanning product codes, taking guided product photos, and exporting consistently named JPEG files. No account or sign-in is required.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

SKU values, product photos, thumbnails, capture presets, and export history are stored in the app's private container on your device. SKU Shot does not upload this content to a developer-operated server. Local data remains until you delete it in the app or remove the app.

2. Camera and Export

Camera access is used only to scan product codes and take product photos. SKU Shot does not request access to your Photos library. Files leave the app only when you choose an action in the iOS share sheet.

3. Advertising

SKU Shot uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to provide ads and regional privacy choices. Depending on your consent choices and device settings, Google may process information such as IP address, coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, app and ad interactions, crash data, and performance data for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and consent management. SKU values and product photos are not sent as ad request fields by SKU Shot.

4. Privacy Choices

Available regional advertising choices can be reopened from the app's Settings screen. You can also manage device privacy settings in iOS Settings.

5. Children

SKU Shot is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when updates are posted.
