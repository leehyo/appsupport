SKU Shot

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

SKU Shot is an iOS app that helps you scan or enter product codes, take product photos, and export organized JPEG files. You can use the app without creating an account.

1. Information Used by the App

Product codes, photos, filenames, capture settings, and recent activity may be stored in the app's private storage on your device. SKU Shot does not operate a server that receives this content.

2. Camera and Local Storage

Camera access is used to scan product codes and take product photos. SKU Shot does not request access to your full Photos library. App data remains on your device unless you choose to export it or remove the app.

3. Sharing and Export

Files are shared only when you choose an export action. The iOS share sheet may send files to Files, AirDrop, or another app or service that you select. Those destinations handle data under their own privacy practices.

4. Advertising

SKU Shot uses Google AdMob to display ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage available privacy choices. Google may process device and app interaction information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and consent management. SKU Shot does not send product codes or product photos to Google as advertising request fields.

5. Your Choices

You can manage available advertising privacy choices from the app's Settings screen and manage app permissions in iOS Settings. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting it where the app provides that option or by deleting the app.

6. Children

SKU Shot is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or related services change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
