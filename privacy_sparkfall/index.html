Sparkfall

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Sparkfall is a single-player logic puzzle game. No account or sign-in is required. Gameplay progress and preferences are stored on your device.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Stage progress, scores, streaks, best times, tutorial status, and display preferences may be stored locally on your device. Sparkfall does not send this gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

2. Advertising

Sparkfall uses Google AdMob and Google's consent tools to provide banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Depending on your consent choices and device settings, Google may process data such as IP address, coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, app and ad interactions, crash information, and performance information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and consent management.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy

3. Tracking and Privacy Choices

Where required, Sparkfall asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before using data for tracking. You can decline this permission and continue playing. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS Settings and reopen available regional privacy choices from the app's privacy options.

4. Data Sharing and Retention

Sparkfall does not sell personal data. Advertising data processed by Google is handled according to Google's policies and your consent choices. Local gameplay data remains on your device until you remove the app or erase its data. Google determines retention for data processed by its services.

5. Children

Sparkfall is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
