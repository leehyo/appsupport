SeoulChance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

SeoulChance helps you find and manage public service reservations in Seoul. No account or sign-in is required, and the developer does not collect personal information through a developer-operated server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

SeoulChance does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or usage behavior. The app does not use advertising, analytics SDKs, or user tracking.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Preferred districts and categories, the free-program setting, favorites, and cached public reservation information are stored only on your device. This information is removed when you delete the app.

3. Calendar and External Services

Calendar events are presented in Apple's system editor for you to review and save. SeoulChance does not read your existing calendar events.

Public reservation information is loaded from the Seoul Open Data service. The service may process ordinary network connection information such as your IP address, but the developer does not receive or store it. When you choose an official reservation, map, or data-source link, a Seoul Metropolitan Government or Apple service opens and its privacy policy applies.

4. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if app features or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page.
