PaceLedger

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 12, 2026

PaceLedger is a local-first personal ledger app for recording expenses, income, transfers, categories, templates, and app settings that you enter manually. PaceLedger does not require an account, does not connect to banks or card issuers, and does not upload your ledger records to developer servers.

1. Information Used by the App

  • Ledger records: amounts, dates, transaction types, categories, accounts, and notes may be stored on your device.
  • Templates and settings: reusable templates, display currency, category settings, and local app preferences may be stored on your device.
  • Ad delivery state: if ads are enabled, the advertising SDK may process information related to ad requests and ad display.

2. Local Storage

The ledger data you enter is stored locally on your device using app storage. The developer cannot view, recover, or restore your transaction history, notes, categories, templates, or app settings from a server.

You can remove local data through the app's reset controls or by deleting the app from your device.

3. Ads

PaceLedger may use Google AdMob banner ads to support the free app. Google AdMob may process information such as IP address, approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, and performance information for ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and consent management.

PaceLedger does not send your ledger records, account names, categories, templates, or notes to Google AdMob or to developer servers.

4. Children

PaceLedger is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
